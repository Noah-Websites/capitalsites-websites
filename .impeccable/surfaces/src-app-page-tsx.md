# Surface brief: site

Scope: entire CapitalSites marketing site — home, pricing, services, portfolio index, audit, contact, nav, footer, layout. The three concept demos under /portfolio/* keep their own worlds.

Visitor mode: Persuade.

## Direction contract

THESIS: CapitalSites presented the way Apple presents a product: the website plan is the product, shown big, shown on real screens, priced plainly. Refuses the small-agency template (stock hero, icon cards, testimonial carousel) and the previous receipt world the owner rejected as plain.

OWN-WORLD: Full-bleed alternating tiles — true black (#000) and Apple grey (#F5F5F7) — punctuated by vivid colour tiles: CapitalSites red (#E3262F → ember #FF6A3D) and deep navy (#0A1A3F → #1E3A8A). Inter at display weights (600–700, tight -0.03em tracking) for 56–96px headlines; Inter 400 body 17–21px. Apple-style chevron text links ("Learn more ›") in brand red on light, #FF6B6B on dark; pill buttons 980px radius. Product imagery = real screenshots of the three concept sites set in CSS-drawn MacBook and iPhone frames. One gradient signature: red→ember on the "$0" figure only.

STORY: Visitor sees "Your website. Rebuilt." over a laptop showing a real concept site, reads "$0 upfront. $99/month.", taps Get a free audit or See pricing. Scrolling: the $0 tile, speed + one-person tiles, a gallery of the three concept sites in phones, a note from Noah, closing CTA.
